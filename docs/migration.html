Diagnostics

Iowa calibration: read with care

  • Farmland rents fail the cash-rent check in sign. Model rents match the NASS level on average but are higher where NASS rents are lower (correlation −0.44 across counties), because farm jobs per hectare are highest in southern Iowa's livestock and pasture counties.
  • The commuting slope depends on the sample. κλ = 0.021 per minute fits all in-state county pairs. On trips under 60 to 90 minutes it is about 0.05, close to the 0.08 default. Travel-time shocks are sensitive to this choice.
  • The farm wage is labour earnings only (hired-labour compensation per job, applied to proprietors too). Proprietor labour and the BEA vs Census of Agriculture hired-labour gap are open VERIFY items.
  • Dollar figures use one model wage unit = (state non-farm earnings per non-farm worker, 2022).

About the model

Places. The region is a set of counties: one core city, its suburbs and rural counties. Every county has two kinds of jobs, non-farm and farm. Farm output uses labour and farmland; non-farm output uses labour and commercial floor space.

Choices. Each person picks where to live and where to work. Commuting costs rise with travel time, so most people work near home. Where they live depends on wages they can reach, floor rents and local amenities. People change residence slowly, which gives an adjustment path over many periods.

A semi-closed region. The parameter η sets how open the region is to the rest of the country. In this version η = 0: nobody moves in from elsewhere in the country, so a growing city fills from its own countryside. International immigration is an optional, exogenous number of arrivals per period, who settle by the same rules as residents.

When the path stops. The countryside “has enough people for the land” when farmland per farm worker in rural counties has risen by the consolidation target ε (for example 10%). The path stops at that period, at a steady state, or at the horizon, whichever comes first.

Land. Farmland is partly worked by owner-operators and partly rented from landlords. Land income is wealth: a farmer who moves keeps it, so by default it does not hold people in place. Farmland and urban land are priced as the present value of the rents the model produces, which is how the page reports wealth changes for farmers who stay and farmers who leave.

Regions. The synthetic 25-county state has known answers, so every result can be checked. Iowa 2022 uses real county data (99 counties, Polk County as the core) and Iowa estimates of the land share in farming θ_T and the commuting slope κλ. Iowa also has an external zone for the out-of-state homes and jobs of commuters. It is part of the model and of the region totals, but it is not drawn on the map and not counted as rural.

The model is solved in your browser with Python (Pyodide); the page only draws what the solver returns, and hides results whenever the solver fails to converge.
